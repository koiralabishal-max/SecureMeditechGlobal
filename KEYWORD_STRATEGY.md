## 1. International B2B Medical Buyer Keyword Strategy

### Target Buyer Personas & Search Intent
1. **Hospital & Health Ministry Procurement Directors**: Search commercial & wholesale terms (*"wholesale medical supplies"*, *"hospital supplies wholesale"*, *"CE 0123 CVP kit supplier"*, *"ISO 13485 closed suction catheter"*). Require MOQ transparency, lead times (3–5 days / 24–48h emergency), Incoterms (`FOB`, `CIF`, `DDP`), and local health authority documentation (SAHPRA, Kenya PPB, Rwanda FDA, NAFDAC, CDSCO, Nepal DDA).
2. **National & Regional Medical Distributors**: Search multi-category supplier & compliance terms (*"international medical equipment supplier"*, *"medical consumables supplier"*, *"surgical consumables manufacturer"*). Need bundled product divisions, export carton specs, Certificate of Origin (COO), and Certificate of Free Sale (FSC).
3. **Medical Device Brand Owners & OEM Buyers**: Search manufacturing & branding intent (*"OEM medical device manufacturer"*, *"private label medical supplies"*, *"ISO Class 7 cleanroom medical molding"*, *"custom surgical OT combo pack OEM"*). Need DFM/CAD prototyping, sterilization validation (ISO 11135/11137), and realistic category MOQs (500 to 10,000 units).
4. **Clinical Laboratory & Diagnostics Managers**: Search capital & analytical equipment terms (*"laboratory equipment supplier"*, *"diagnostic equipment supplier"*, *"automated clinical biochemistry analyzer CE IVD"*). Need throughput specs (`400 T/H`), cold-chain logistics (`2°C–8°C`), and NIST calibration.

### Anti-Cannibalization Architecture
- **Commercial wholesale intent** maps to `/` (umbrella), `/products` (master catalog), `/products/category/[slug]` (8 distinct clinical divisions), and `/products/[id]` (18 SKU technical pages).
- **OEM & private-label contract intent** maps cleanly to `/oem-manufacturing` (cleanroom manufacturing workflow & MOQs) and `/services` (6 end-to-end B2B lifecycle services).
- **Trust, regulatory & corporate supplier intent** maps to `/about` (ISO 13485, CE MDR 2017/745, regional corridors, partner brands) and `/contact` (24/7 RFQ desk, Incoterms 2020, direct channels).

## 5. Internal Linking & Crawl Architecture
1. **Top Navigation & Mobile Drawer (`Navbar.tsx`)**: Links directly to `/`, `/products`, all 8 `/products/category/[slug]` divisions, `/oem-manufacturing`, `/services`, `/about`, `/#price-calculator`, and `/contact`.
2. **Master Catalog (`/products`)**: Links into all 8 `/products/category/[slug]` division hubs, all 18 `/products/[id]` detail pages, `/oem-manufacturing`, and `/contact`.
3. **Category Hubs (`/products/category/[slug]`)**: Link down to every category SKU (`/products/[id]`), across to 3 related categories, to supporting `/services#<slug>`, to `/oem-manufacturing`, and to `/contact`.
4. **Product Detail Pages (`/products/[id]`)**: Include structured `BreadcrumbList` back to `/products/category/[slug]`, up to 3 related SKUs in the same category, 2 matching B2B service cards (`/services#<slug>`), `/oem-manufacturing` private-label callout, and `/contact` + WhatsApp + `/#price-calculator` conversion links.
5. **Footer Directory (`Footer.tsx`)**: Exposes all 8 category pages, 6 OEM/service destinations, `/about`, `/products`, `/services`, `/contact`, FAQ, and the embedded Price Estimation Calculator across every page of the site.
